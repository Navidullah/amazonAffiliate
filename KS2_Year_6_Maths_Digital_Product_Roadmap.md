# Year 6 KS2 Maths Digital Product Roadmap

## Purpose

Build a professional Year 6 KS2 Maths digital-product library on the website. The goal is to sell original maths workbooks, worksheets, SATs practice papers, mock tests, revision programmes, and bundles for parents, tutors, and students in the UK market.

> Important: Create original questions, explanations, illustrations, layouts, and solutions. Do not reproduce copyrighted commercial resources or official SATs papers/questions.

# 25. Important Content Rules

All resources should:

- Be original
- Use clear UK English
- Use age-appropriate language
- Use UK mathematical terminology
- Be visually engaging
- Include worked examples
- Include answers
- Include explanations where appropriate
- Progress from easy → standard → challenge
- Include reasoning and problem-solving
- Be printable
- Work well in PDF format
- Have consistent branding
- Be suitable for home learning and tutoring

Do not copy:

- Official SATs questions
- Commercial worksheets
- Other websites' workbooks
- Commercial question banks
- Copyrighted illustrations
- Competitors' layouts

Use the official England National Curriculum and KS2 assessment framework as the curriculum reference, then create original educational content.
